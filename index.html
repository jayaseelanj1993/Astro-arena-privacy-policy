Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026 · Last updated: 25 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Astro Animation Studio ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use the Astro Report Android app and the website at reports.astroanimationstudio.com (together, the "App"). Astro Report lets site and project teams write, save, download and share site reports such as weekly, daily and monthly progress reports, safety reports and quality reports.

In short

  • We collect only what is needed to run your account and store your reports.
  • Your password is stored only in encrypted (hashed) form. We never see it.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • The App shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use device identifiers. You can control ad personalisation.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and all your reports at any time.

1. Information we collect

a) Information you give us

DataWhenWhy
Full name, username, email addressWhen you register or an administrator creates your accountTo create and identify your account, and to contact you about it
Phone number (optional)If you choose to add it at registrationSo your organisation's administrator can reach you
PasswordWhen you register or change itTo sign you in securely. Stored only as a one-way hash (bcrypt), never in readable form
Report contentWhen you create or edit a reportTo save, display and export your reports. This can include project names, locations, dates, manpower numbers, progress notes, safety and quality details, and names and designations entered in sign-off sections

Please only enter information about other people (for example, names in a toolbox talk attendance list or sign-off block) when you are allowed to do so by your organisation.

b) Information collected automatically

  • Sign-in records: the date and time of your last sign-in, and a count of failed sign-in attempts, used to protect your account from password-guessing.
  • Technical data: your IP address and basic request information (such as time and the page or function used) are processed by our server for security, rate-limiting and troubleshooting. These are kept in short-lived server logs.
  • Advertising data: see section 3.

c) Information stored only on your device

  • A sign-in token, so you stay signed in.
  • Your last-used username, if you tick "Remember me".
  • An unsaved draft of the report you are editing, so your work is not lost if the app closes.
  • PDF and CSV files you choose to download, saved to your device's Downloads folder.

This information stays on your device and is not sent to us. Signing out removes the sign-in token, and uninstalling the app removes the rest of the app's stored data (downloaded files stay in your Downloads folder until you delete them).

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, call logs or SMS.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the App: create your account, sign you in, and save, show and export your reports.
  • To let your organisation's administrator manage accounts (approve, disable, reset passwords) and view reports, where your organisation uses the App this way.
  • To keep the App secure: detect and block repeated failed sign-ins and misuse.
  • To fix problems and improve the App.
  • To show advertising that helps keep the App free (see below).
  • To meet legal obligations.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android app shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads and measure their performance, Google may collect and use information from your device, such as the device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this information in line with its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Where required by law (for example, in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the App asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time from the "Ad privacy settings" link in the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Security & privacy → Privacy → Ads).

Your report content is never shared with advertisers or used for advertising.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share information only:

  • Within your organisation: administrators of the App can see user accounts and reports in order to manage them.
  • With service providers who help us run the App, under appropriate safeguards: our server hosting provider (GoDaddy) and Google (AdMob advertising).
  • When you choose to share: PDF or CSV files you download can be shared by you with anyone you choose. What happens to them afterwards is under your control.
  • For legal reasons: if required by law, court order or a valid request from authorities, or to protect the rights, safety and security of users and the App.
  • Business transfer: if the App is transferred to another owner, your information may be transferred under the same protections described here.

5. Storage, security and international transfer

  • Account and report data is stored on our server hosted by GoDaddy in Singapore. If you use the App from another country, your information will be transferred to and processed there.
  • All data sent between the App and our server is encrypted in transit with HTTPS (TLS).
  • Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes. Sign-in tokens can be cancelled by changing your password.
  • Accounts are locked for a short time after repeated wrong passwords, and access to data is limited by role (user or administrator).
  • We keep regular backups to protect against data loss.

No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information. Please keep your password private and use a strong one.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account and reports: kept while your account exists. You can delete individual reports in the app at any time.
  • After account deletion: your account and all your reports are removed from our live database straight away. Copies in backups are overwritten within 30 days.
  • Server logs: kept for up to 30 days for security and troubleshooting, then deleted.

7. Delete your account and data

You can ask us to delete your Astro Report account and all data linked to it at any time:

  1. Send an email to jayaseelan.js97@gmail.com with the subject "Astro Report – Delete my account".
  2. Send it from the email address registered on your account, and include your username.
  3. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 7 days.

What is deleted: your name, username, email, phone number, password hash, sign-in records and every report you created. What may be kept: nothing, except backup copies that are overwritten within 30 days, and anything we are required by law to keep. If your account was created by your organisation, you can also ask your organisation's administrator, who can delete it directly.

To delete only some reports, open the report list in the app and tap Delete on each one.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or the EU/UK GDPR), you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your information (see section 7);
  • withdraw consent, including consent for personalised ads;
  • object to or restrict certain use of your information;
  • receive a copy of your reports (you can download them as PDF or CSV at any time);
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We may need to confirm your identity first.

9. App permissions

  • Internet: to connect to our server, sign you in, save your reports and load ads.
  • Advertising ID (through Google Mobile Ads): used by Google AdMob to show and measure ads.

Downloads are saved through Android's standard Downloads system and do not need access to your other files.

10. Children's privacy

Astro Report is a work tool made for adults working on construction and project sites. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. If the changes are significant, we will also tell you in the app. Continuing to use the App after an update means you accept the updated policy.
